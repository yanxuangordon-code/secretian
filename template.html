Secretian

Turn normal English into a secret language, and back again.

You can also drag an image onto this box, or paste a screenshot with Ctrl/Cmd + V.

Every word in the dictionary (plus lots of gaming words and names) is swapped for a different word. Anything not in the word list, like numbers or very new slang, is left unchanged. Everything runs in your browser and your messages are never sent anywhere. Reading an image also happens on your device (the image is never uploaded), but the first time you use it, the page downloads a free text-reading tool of about 8 MB.
